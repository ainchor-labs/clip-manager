Privacy policy

Clip Manager · last updated October 28, 2026

Who uses it

Clip Manager is a private, self-hosted tool. Its only user is the owner of the GooseMode YouTube channel, who signs in with their own Google account. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube upload and management (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the owner's own clips to their channel, add caption tracks and post a comment linking the full stream.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time, likes and subscriber counts for the owner's channel and videos.

How it is stored

The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's private home server, and the statistics are stored in a database on the same server. Nothing is sent to any other service.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is only shown to the channel owner, inside the tool. Clip Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube inside the tool, which deletes the stored token, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Statistics can be deleted by removing them from the server's database.
